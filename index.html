Seven Coins

Seven letters. One red coin.

Two teams take turns buzzing in to build words from the coins on the board. Every turn gives 35 seconds to buzz and type, and the red coin in the middle awards +3 bonus points.

The host opens a game and shares a four-letter code. Everyone else opens this page on their own phone or laptop and joins a team. Players without a device can be added by hand in the lobby.

Tap a coin to shuffle. A new set is dealt for each game.

The rules

The coins
Use only the seven letters on the board. Repeat any letter as often as you need.
The red coin (bonus)
It sits in the middle, circled. Using it is optional, but any word with the red letter earns +3 bonus points — just like the Queen in carrom.
Length
Three letters or more. Two-letter words don't count. Names of people, places and brands don't count either.
The buzzer & clock
Each team gets 35 seconds per turn. Anyone eligible on that team can hit the buzzer to claim the turn and type a word.
3-chance cooldown
Whoever hits the buzzer sits out the buzzer for their team's next 3 chances so everyone gets a turn.
Words are used up
A word can only be played once per round, by either team.
New rounds
A round keeps going, even when words get scarce, until the host closes it and deals fresh letters. Scores carry over from round to round.
